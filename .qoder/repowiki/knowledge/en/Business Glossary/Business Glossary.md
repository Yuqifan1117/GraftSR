### ODTSR
- Definition：One-Step Text-to-Image Super-Resolution - the core research task being implemented in this project, focusing on single-step image super-resolution using text guidance and reference images.
- Aliases：one-step-sr、text-guided-sr

### Qwen-Image-Edit
- Definition：The base model architecture used for image editing tasks, specifically version 2511 which serves as the foundation for the ODTSR training pipeline.
- Aliases：qwen-edit、qwen-image-edit-2511

### Wan2.1-T2V
- Definition：Text-to-Video model from Wan-AI series used as a component in the ODTSR pipeline, specifically the 1.3B parameter variant.
- Aliases：wan-video、wan2.1-t2v-1.3b

### LQ/HQ
- Definition：Low Quality / High Quality image pairs used in super-resolution training, where LQ represents degraded input images and HQ represents target high-quality outputs.
- Aliases：low-quality、high-quality、degraded-images

### REF
- Definition：Reference images used as additional conditioning input alongside LQ images for guided super-resolution, providing style or content transfer capabilities.
- Aliases：reference-images、ref-crop

### Mask
- Definition：Segmentation masks generated by SAM3 for identifying regions in images, used for targeted editing and fidelity control in the super-resolution process.
- Aliases：sam3-mask、region-mask、fidelity-mask

### Neblua Cluster
- Definition：Internal cluster management system previously used for submitting training jobs, characterized by queue-based job submission, OSS/NAS storage integration, and worker count configuration.
- Aliases：nebulactl、cluster-job、queue-system

### Benchmark Datasets
- Definition：Standard evaluation datasets including synthetic benchmarks (synthetic_250), real-world benchmarks (realworld, realworld_100), and public benchmarks (RealSR-V3, drealsr) used for model evaluation.
- Aliases：eval-datasets、test-benchmarks、benchmark_sets
