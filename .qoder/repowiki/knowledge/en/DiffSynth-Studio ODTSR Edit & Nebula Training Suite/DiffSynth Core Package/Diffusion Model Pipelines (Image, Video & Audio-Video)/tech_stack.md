PyTorch with `einops` for tensor reshaping, `tqdm` for progress bars, HuggingFace `transformers` tokenizers/processors (CLIPTokenizer, T5TokenizerFast, Wav2Vec2Processor, AutoProcessor, Gemma3Processor). Scheduling uses the in-house `FlowMatchScheduler` with model-specific presets (FLUX.1, Wan, LTX-2, Z-Image). VRAM management leverages `AutoWrappedLinear` and gradient checkpointing from `..core.vram.layers` and `..core.gradient_checkpoint_forward`.
