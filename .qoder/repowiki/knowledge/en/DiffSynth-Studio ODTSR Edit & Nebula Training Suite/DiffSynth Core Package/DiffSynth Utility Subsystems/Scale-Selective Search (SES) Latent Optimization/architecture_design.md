Single-file module (`ses.py`) exposing one public entry point `ses_search` re-exported through `__init__.py`. The algorithm is layered into three cooperating functions: `split_dwt` performs a 2D discrete wavelet transform via PyWavelets to separate each latent into a low-frequency subband and fixed high-frequency detail coefficients; `reconstruct_dwt` inverts the transform given a candidate low-frequency patch and the preserved high-frequency details; `ses_search` orchestrates an elite-based CMA-style search over the flattened low-frequency coefficient space, maintaining a running mean `mu` and variance `sigma_sq`, sampling `popsize` candidates per generation, evaluating them through the user-supplied `objective_reward_fn`, retaining the top `k_elites`, and updating the distribution parameters until `total_eval_budget` evaluations are exhausted. Dependency direction is strictly inward: the module depends only on `torch`, `pywt`, `numpy`, and `tqdm`, with no imports from other diffsynth packages.
