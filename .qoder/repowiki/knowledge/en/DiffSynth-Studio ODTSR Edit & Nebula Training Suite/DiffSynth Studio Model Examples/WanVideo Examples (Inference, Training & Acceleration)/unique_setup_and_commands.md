Each training `.sh` script first runs `modelscope download --dataset DiffSynth-Studio/diffsynth_example_dataset --include "wanvideo/<variant>/*" --local_dir ./data/diffsynth_example_dataset` to fetch the dataset, then launches training with `accelerate launch --config_file examples/wanvideo/model_training/full/accelerate_config_14B.yaml examples/wanvideo/model_training/train.py` passing dataset paths, model IDs, resolution, learning rate, epochs, and trainable modules. Low-VRAM variants live under `model_inference_low_vram/` and share the same script names as their high-VRAM counterparts.
