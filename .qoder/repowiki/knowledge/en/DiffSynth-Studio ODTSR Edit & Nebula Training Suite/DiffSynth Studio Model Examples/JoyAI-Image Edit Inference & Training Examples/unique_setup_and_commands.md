Datasets are downloaded via `modelscope.dataset_snapshot_download(dataset_id="DiffSynth-Studio/diffsynth_example_dataset", allow_file_pattern="joyai_image/JoyAI-Image-Edit/*")`. Full training runs a two-step process: first `--task sft:data_process` to cache inputs, then `--task sft:train` with `accelerate launch --config_file .../accelerate_config_zero3.yaml`. LoRA training mirrors this pattern but adds `--lora_base_model dit --lora_target_modules ... --lora_rank 32`. Low-VRAM inference passes per-model VRAM configs (`offload_dtype`, `onload_device`, `vram_limit`) to `ModelConfig`.
