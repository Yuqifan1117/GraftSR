Training is launched via shell scripts under `model_training/{full,lora}/` (e.g. `Qwen-Image.sh`) that call `accelerate launch` with an accompanying `accelerate_config.yaml` (ZeRO-2/ZeRO-3/offload variants provided). Inference scripts are single-file Python programs runnable directly; low-VRAM variants require setting `vram_limit` in GB. GAN experiments are driven by `train_gan*.py` scripts reading parameters from `configs/*.yaml` files.
