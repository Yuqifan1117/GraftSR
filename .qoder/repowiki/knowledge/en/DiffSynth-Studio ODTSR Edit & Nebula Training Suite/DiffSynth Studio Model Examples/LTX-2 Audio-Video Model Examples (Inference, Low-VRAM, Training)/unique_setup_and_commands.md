Training uses a two-step `accelerate launch` workflow driven by the `.sh` files in `model_training/full/` and `model_training/lora/`: first run with `--task sft:data_process` to build a cached dataset, then re-run with `--task sft:train` pointing at the cache directory. Data is downloaded via `modelscope download --dataset DiffSynth-Studio/diffsynth_example_dataset --include "ltx2/*"`. Low-VRAM inference passes `vram_limit=torch.cuda.mem_get_info("cuda")[1]/(1024**3)-0.5` to `from_pretrained`.
