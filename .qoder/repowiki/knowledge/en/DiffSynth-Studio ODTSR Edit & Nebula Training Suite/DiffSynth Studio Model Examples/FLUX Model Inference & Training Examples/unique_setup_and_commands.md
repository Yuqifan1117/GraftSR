Each training shell script first downloads example data via `modelscope download --dataset DiffSynth-Studio/diffsynth_example_dataset --include "flux/<MODEL>/*" --local_dir ./data/diffsynth_example_dataset`, then launches training with `accelerate launch --config_file examples/flux/model_training/<variant>/accelerate_config.yaml examples/flux/model_training/train.py ...`. Full fine-tuning uses `--trainable_models dit` while LoRA training sets `--lora_base_model dit`, `--lora_target_modules`, `--lora_rank 32`, and `--align_to_opensource_format`. Low-VRAM inference adds a `vram_config` dict specifying offload/onload/preparing/computation dtypes and devices plus `vram_limit=torch.cuda.mem_get_info("cuda")[1]/(1024**3)-0.5`.
